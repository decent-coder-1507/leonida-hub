Is GTA 6 on PC?

No. GTA 6 is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date for one.

Some reports suggest a PC release could come in 2027. Treat that as unconfirmed until Rockstar says so. Xbox Cloud Gaming also does not stream GTA 6 to PC.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Sources: Zap-Hosting, Nacionale.

Does GTA 6 have an online mode?

Not at launch. GTA 6 released as a single-player game. No online mode, release date or crossplay has been announced.

The existing GTA Online, which runs on GTA 5, continues to be supported.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Source: GTASide.

GTA 6 price in India

The Standard Edition is listed at ₹5,999 and the Ultimate Edition at ₹7,499 on the PlayStation Store in India. The US Standard Edition price is $79.99.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Source: Business Today. Prices can change, so check the store before buying.

Playing without owning a console

You cannot play GTA 6 on a PC or phone on its own. These are the realistic options in India:

  • Gaming cafe: PS5 time usually costs about ₹100 to ₹200 per hour.
  • Renting a PS5: about ₹500 to ₹700 per day, or ₹4,000 to ₹5,000 per week. You still need the game.
  • Xbox One with cloud streaming: Xbox streams GTA 6 to Xbox One consoles for players who own the game and have cloud playtime. Check that the option appears on your account before buying.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Sources: Gaming Nation, Cashify, Xbox Wire.

How the story is structured

The story has five chapters. Most guides count 91 mandatory missions: 22, 11, 26, 25 and 7 across the chapters. Other guides count up to 97, mainly because they treat the prologue and some optional missions differently.

This page does not list mission names, to keep it spoiler-free.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Sources: G2A News, Technerdiness.

What 100% completion needs

You do not need to collect everything. The game asks for a set number in each category, for example 15 of 18 collectible card sets and 30 of 40 unique races.

Launch-week guides do not agree on every count, so check yours against the pause-menu checklist. The 100% tracker lists each requirement and saves your progress.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Sources: PowerPyx, Push Square.

Rules for posting GTA 6 videos

Normal gameplay videos are generally tolerated, but story spoilers get taken down. Rockstar's video policy covers all its games.

  • Likely to be removed: the ending, standalone cutscene clips, big story reveals, and in-game TV shows posted on their own.
  • Music: licensed radio songs can trigger copyright claims, so many creators turn the radio off.
  • Earning from videos: the policy describes non-commercial fan use and does not explicitly grant permission to monetise, although monetised gameplay videos have been common for years.

Last checked 20 Nov 2026. Sources: Rockstar Games, TheGamer.
